# 247ROI Goals and Operating Cadence

## North Star

247ROI should become a credible, findable, conversion-ready business systems brand that turns qualified attention into AI Opportunity Audit starts, booked conversations, and practical systems work.

## 90-Day Goals

1. Prove measurement: public-site page views, CTA clicks, phone/email clicks, audit starts, gate submits, report unlocks, and lead quality are visible in the command center.
2. Build qualified acquisition: core commercial pages are live, indexed, internally linked, and improving from search/AI visibility evidence.
3. Earn AI visibility: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI surfaces, and Bing can correctly describe 247ROI as a business systems, AI automation, dashboard, internal app, and AI agent builder.
4. Improve conversion: the homepage, services pages, and `/hire` produce enough tracked behavior to identify friction and justify page changes.
5. Add proof and authority: publish useful diagnostic assets, workflow examples, teardown content, and legitimate citations/backlinks.

## Operating Loops

### Daily

- Check command-center event health.
- Review new audit starts, report unlocks, calls, email clicks, and any broken tracking.
- Ship or queue one concrete improvement if a blocker is obvious.

### Weekly

- Review search baselines, page coverage, sitemap/llms coverage, and live site events.
- Run AI answer checks for tracked prompts.
- Pick one acquisition action, one authority action, and one conversion action.
- Update the scorecard with proof, not vibes.

### Monthly

- Refresh keyword baselines and page priorities.
- Review Search Console and Bing data once connected.
- Decide which pages to improve, consolidate, expand, or stop prioritizing.
- Build one proof asset or diagnostic resource worth linking to.

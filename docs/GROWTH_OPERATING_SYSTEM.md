# 247ROI Growth Operating System

247ROI needs an autonomous website operator, not a passive analytics dashboard.

The target system improves:

- qualified traffic
- AI visibility and entity clarity
- trust and proof
- website conversion
- AI Opportunity Audit starts and completions
- lead quality
- authority and backlink quality

## Four Operating Loops

### Acquisition Loop

Purpose: earn qualified attention.

Inputs:

- keyword clusters
- SERPs
- competitor pages and posts
- AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
- Reddit/forum/customer language
- adjacent high-performing content frameworks

Outputs:

- service pages
- answer pages
- comparison pages
- articles
- social post frameworks
- internal links
- search snippets and metadata

### Conversion Loop

Purpose: turn attention into audit starts, report unlocks, calls, and qualified conversations.

Inputs:

- page views
- CTA clicks
- phone and email clicks
- audit starts
- audit completions
- report unlocks
- drop-off points
- B's judgment on clarity and offer quality

Outputs:

- stronger homepage copy
- clearer CTAs
- reduced funnel friction
- better proof sections
- better audit framing
- page-specific conversion fixes

### Authority / GEO Loop

Purpose: make 247ROI easy for search engines and AI answer engines to understand and cite.

Inputs:

- schema coverage
- sitemap coverage
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`
- internal link coverage
- entity consistency
- answer-engine prompts
- mentions, directories, backlinks, podcasts, partner pages

Outputs:

- schema fixes
- entity and About-page improvements
- answer-style pages
- authority targets
- proof assets
- AI visibility checks

### Taste Loop

Purpose: avoid generic AI-shaped content.

Inputs:

- competitor examples
- B's corrections and opinions
- sales objections
- real business-owner phrasing
- cultural/business trends
- high-performing adjacent posts

Outputs:

- hook frameworks
- post templates
- content quality gates
- banned phrase list
- voice memory
- examples of "rewrite because bland"

## Weekly Operator Cycle

Every week, Athena should answer:

1. What shipped?
2. What ranked, got indexed, or earned impressions?
3. What got traffic?
4. What converted?
5. What failed or got ignored?
6. What changed in the market or competitor set?
7. What do AI systems say 247ROI does?
8. What should be built, improved, or stopped next?

## Content Rules

- Hook first.
- Use short lines.
- Avoid walls of text.
- Do not use generic AI words: "delve", "testament", "revolutionize", "tapestry", "in conclusion".
- Every page section or post must provide utility, proof, emotional resonance, entertainment payoff, or sharp clarity.
- Extract structure from competitor examples without copying wording.
- Prefer plain English business pain over abstract AI language.
